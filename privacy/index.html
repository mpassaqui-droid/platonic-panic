What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, skins, music unlocked), your settings (language, sound, haptics, reduce motion), the birth year you enter at first launch, and the identifiers of your store purchases, so the same purchase is never counted twice. These files live only in the game's private storage on your phone. We never receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game, and the RESET PROGRESS button clears your progress at any time.

The birth year is asked once, with no default answer. It is checked on the device only, to choose the ad settings (see below). It is never sent to us or to Google.

Ads (free version only)

The free version offers rewarded videos from Google AdMob: you only see one if you tap to watch it, for example for a continue or bonus gems. There are no banners and no forced ads, and every ad is limited to the G rating.

Players under the age of digital consent (13 in most countries, 13 to 16 in the EU, EEA and UK depending on the country, 16 if the country is unknown), or who did not answer the age question: every ad request is non-personalized and tagged as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR). No consent message, no tracking request, and the advertising identifier is not used for advertising.

Adult players: ads are personalized only if you agree. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks you first. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks whether the game may track you (App Tracking Transparency); if you refuse, the advertising identifier stays hidden. If you agree, Google may use your device's advertising identifier and your interactions with ads to show ads that match your interests, including across other apps (this is what Apple calls tracking). If you do not agree, ads are non-personalized.

In every case, to load and show an ad, the Google Mobile Ads software inside the game connects to Google. Google may process your IP address (which reveals an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, to deliver the ad, count it, limit how often it appears and prevent fraud. Google acts under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

You can change your choice at any time: in the game, Settings, PRIVACY CHOICES (asks your age again and, where it applies, shows Google's consent message); on iPhone, Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking; on Android, Settings, Google, Ads, to reset or delete the advertising ID.

Once you buy the full game, the ads software is no longer started and no ad request is sent.

Purchases

The full game, gem packs and skins are sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that the purchase is valid.

Children

Platonic Panic is made for everyone, children included. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, of any age. A neutral age question at first launch (no default answer, nothing suggested) decides the ad settings: below the age of digital consent, ads are always child-directed and non-personalized, with no consent or tracking request. Purchases always go through the store's own confirmation, which parents can protect with the parental controls of iOS (Screen Time) or Google Play (purchase authentication, Family Link).

Permissions

The game uses the internet only for ads and purchases, vibration for haptics, and the motion sensors for GYRO mode (read on the device, never sent anywhere). It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and object. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing we can return or delete, but you can write to us at munay@responia.ai and we will answer within one month. To withdraw consent to personalized ads, use PRIVACY CHOICES or your phone's settings (see Ads). For data processed by Google for ads, use Google's privacy tools. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before the new version is released and the date above will change.
