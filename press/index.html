About the game

Your hero walks on its own. You have one move: tap to flip gravity. Chain neon explosions, dodge spikes and traps, and survive 140 levels across 7 episodes before the impossible SUPERNOVA finale.

  • 140 levels in 7 episodes, plus the SUPERNOVA finale
  • A new daily level every day
  • GYRO mode: tilt the phone to steer, flick it to flip gravity
  • Races against friends on the same Wi-Fi
  • Neon skins, star coins, perfect runs and crowns
  • An original soundtrack: two albums, 22 tracks, with an in-game jukebox
  • No account, plays offline, rewarded ads only (never forced)
